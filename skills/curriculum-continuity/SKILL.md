# Curriculum continuity

Plan across lessons from the course schedule and saved work. In teacher course preparation, treat earlier scheduled lessons as taught background and build the next lesson from their concepts. In learner modes, use the durable Timeline when available to adapt to observed progress.

## Course progression

Map the concept dependency chain and identify load-bearing prerequisites, notation dependencies and likely bottlenecks. Preserve the teacher's goals, calendar and source boundaries. Ask only for missing constraints that would materially change sequence, assessment or workload; do not impose an unnecessary multi-round interview.

Make each lesson sufficiently self-contained to teach, while connecting it to earlier content and later use. Place a compact retrieval hook where prior learning matters, then advance to the new material.

## Spiral revisits

For every planned revisit, define a Spiral Contract:

- concept being revisited;
- prior encounter in the course sequence;
- what remains stable;
- what becomes harder or more connected;
- new representation, relationship, abstraction, formalization, transfer distance or boundary;
- learner task;
- evidence that will demonstrate the new level.

Use one or more explicit progression dimensions: complexity, relationship, abstraction, formalization, representation, transfer or boundary. A new title, different numbers or the same explanation later in the calendar does not constitute progression.

Choose whether to keep the earlier model, add a layer, reorganize it or transfer it to a new domain. Surface a prior simplification when it stops being valid. Let the new task require the added structure.

## Runtime boundaries

In Course Builder, use `state`, approved semester revisions, prerequisites, `courseGoalsCovered`, `revisits` and earlier lesson drafts to plan the sequence. Earlier scheduled lessons provide the assumed background for subsequent lessons. In learner modes, use available Host tools for durable observations; conversation memory alone is not the source of truth.

Before saving, check for skipped prerequisites, mechanical repetition, overloaded sessions, goals that disappear across the term and revisits with no changed performance. Record gaps rather than fabricating history.
